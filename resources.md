## Continuing Education (CE)

### CDC TRAIN / CDC Learning Lab
Free continuing education courses for healthcare professionals from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Topics include infection control, emergency preparedness, and public health practice.

- Audience: All healthcare workers
- Cost: Free
- Official source: https://www.cdc.gov/training/

### OpenWHO
Free online courses from the World Health Organization for health workers and responders, including outbreak response, infection prevention, and clinical care.

- Audience: All healthcare workers
- Cost: Free
- Official source: https://openwho.org

### American Nurses Association (ANA) Free CE
Free continuing education webinars and courses for nurses. Some offerings are ANA-member benefits; others are open to all nurses.

- Audience: Nurses
- Cost: Free
- Official source: https://www.nursingworld.org

## Scholarships & Grants

### HRSA Nurse Corps Scholarship Program
Scholarships for nursing students in exchange for service at a critical shortage facility, plus loan repayment for eligible nurses.

- Audience: Nursing students and nurses
- Cost: Free
- Official source: https://www.hrsa.gov/nurse-corps

### ANA Foundation Scholarships and Grants
Scholarships, research grants, and awards for nurses advancing their education or practice through the American Nurses Foundation.

- Audience: Nurses
- Cost: Free
- Official source: https://www.nursingworld.org/the-foundation/

## Printables & Handouts

### CDC Printable Resources
Free printable fact sheets, posters, and handouts for healthcare settings, covering infection control, immunizations, and workplace health.

- Audience: All healthcare workers

### MedlinePlus Printable Handouts
Free printable health handouts and easy-to-read materials from the National Library of Medicine for patient education and staff reference.

## Support & Crisis Lines

### 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
Free, confidential 24/7 support for anyone in emotional distress or crisis, including healthcare workers. Call or text 988.

- Audience: Anyone in crisis
- Cost: Free
- Official source: https://988lifeline.org

### SAMHSA National Helpline
Free, confidential 24/7 treatment referral and information line for mental health and substance use concerns. Call 1-800-662-HELP (4357).

- Audience: Anyone seeking help
- Cost: Free
- Official source: https://www.samhsa.gov/find-help/national-helpline

### Emotional PPE Project
Connects healthcare workers with free, confidential mental health support from licensed providers. No insurance required.

- Audience: Healthcare workers
- Cost: Free
- Official source: https://www.emotionalppe.org/

### NIOSH Safety and Health Topics
Free workplace safety and health resources for healthcare workers from the National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health, including safe patient handling and stress prevention.

- Audience: All healthcare workers
- Cost: Free
- Official source: https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/
